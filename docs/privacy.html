Privacy policy

This policy describes the Rep workout log. Contact cheejiayuan512@gmail.com.

What is stored

When you log a workout, Rep stores the workout name, exercises, sets, reps, weight, notes, and RPE on your device. Weight is stored in kilograms.

What is not collected

Rep does not have an account. It does not upload your log, and it does not share your log with the developer or with any other service.

How long it stays

The log stays on the device until you delete it. Deleting a workout removes that workout. Deleting the app removes the log from that device.
