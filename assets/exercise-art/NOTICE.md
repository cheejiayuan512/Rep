## Changes

Rep scaled each frame from 512×512 to 256×256 pixels so it can sit on an exercise row. Of the three source frames, the heavier one (thicker, more opaque white lines) is left out, and the other two play in their original order. Where the feet and the body had both slid by the same small amount, that frame is shifted so the figure stays in place. The frames are not recolored or redrawn.
